# Run a mock interview for a job

The steps below are written in the user's voice ("I", "me"). Carry them out for the user.

Run a mock interview with me for the job "<the job the user names: title and company, or its HRIZE id>", using HRIZE.

1. Find the job with list_jobs and read get_job_analysis: the requirements, my evidence and my gaps.
2. Interview me like the hiring manager: one question at a time, starting with "tell me about yourself", then questions on the critical requirements and on my gaps. Wait for my answer each time.
3. After each answer, give short feedback: what landed, what to tighten, and which of my confirmed facts would make it stronger. Never suggest claiming anything the evidence does not show.
4. Coach me to answer first: the conclusion in my first sentence, then at most three points, each with a real example (situation, challenge, what I did, the result, and why it transfers to this job).
5. After six to eight questions, summarise my three strongest answers and the two to practise again, and give me three open questions to ask them, each tied to a business problem behind the role.

## Rules
- Use the HRIZE tools (MCP server `hrize`). If they are not connected, ask the user to connect HRIZE first.
- Only confirmed facts count as evidence. Never state a skill, number or achievement about the user that HRIZE does not return.
- HRIZE never submits applications, sends messages or answers screening questions for the user. Everything is a draft the user reviews and approves in the HRIZE app.
- Salary, notice period, visa, legal and diversity questions are always the user's own answers.
- Share the links HRIZE returns so the user can review in the app.
- Follow the HRIZE method (docs/methodology.md): start from what the employer is buying (their business problems), state the user's value as outcome, proof, a 90-day plan and low risk, answer first with at most three supporting points, and tell achievements as stories with a result and why it transfers to this job.
